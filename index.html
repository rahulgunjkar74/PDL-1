Types of Monitoring Tests

Learn more about the key health parameters used in your project for disease detection and preventive care.

Blood Pressure Test

Measures the pressure of blood flowing through arteries.

Blood pressure testing helps identify hypertension and early cardiovascular risk, supporting preventive treatment and routine monitoring.

Blood Sugar Test

Analyzes glucose levels present in the blood.

Blood sugar values help assess diabetic risk and metabolic balance, making them essential for continuous health screening.

Cholesterol Test

Measures fat levels present in the bloodstream.

Cholesterol monitoring helps identify cardiovascular risk and encourages healthier lifestyle adjustments.

Healthy Lifestyle Tips

These tips make the page look richer while staying relevant to your healthcare project.

🥗 Healthy Diet

Eat a balanced diet with fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein, and healthy fats. A healthy diet supports stable sugar, cholesterol, and blood pressure levels.

🏃 Regular Exercise

Daily physical activity helps improve cardiovascular health, metabolism, and mental wellbeing. It also supports better long-term disease prevention.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Proper hydration supports digestion, circulation, and overall body function. Drinking enough water daily helps maintain better energy and health balance.
